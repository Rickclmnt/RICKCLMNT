PATRICK'S THINKING MACHINE

PTM.

A different approach to artificial intelligence.

PTM is a deterministic reasoning system built around structured knowledge, explicit rules, epistemic evaluation, and controlled language expression. Rather than generating an answer based solely on linguistic probability, PTM is designed to process information through defined stages and produce responses grounded in what its knowledge system can establish.

01 / THE APPROACH

How PTM thinks.

PTM separates the process of understanding a question from the process of reasoning about it and expressing an answer. Each stage has a defined responsibility.

Input is interpreted and converted into a structured representation. The reasoning system then examines relevant knowledge, applies rules, evaluates possible conclusions, and determines what can legitimately be communicated.

This separation is important. A conclusion is not automatically treated as a fact simply because the system can produce a sentence about it.

02 / EPISTEMIC EVALUATION

Not every conclusion is a fact.

PTM distinguishes between different epistemic states. This allows it to communicate the status of a conclusion rather than presenting every response with the same level of certainty.

Fact Established within the knowledge system.
Inference Derived through reasoning from available information.
Hypothesis A possible explanation that remains unconfirmed.
Unknown Insufficient information to establish an answer.

The distinction is intended to prevent unsupported conclusions from silently becoming established knowledge. Unknown information remains unknown until something provides a basis for resolving it.
