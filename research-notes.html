Aether Cognitive Systems

Bounding uncertainty without a probability model

Testing whether a deterministic reasoning system can express "I don't know" in a structured way, without falling back on a statistical confidence score borrowed from the systems Aether is trying not to be.

Energy Brick Materials / Energy

Load-bearing capacity under embedded cell density

Measuring how much energy-storage density can be packed into a structural composite before mechanical performance starts to degrade, and where the acceptable trade-off sits.

ALIS Legal Intelligence

Auditability as a design constraint, not a feature

Investigating what changes in a legal-reasoning system's architecture when every output has to be traceable back to an explicit rule, rather than added as an explanation after the fact.

Failed / Abandoned Approaches

What didn't work.

Failure is part of the archive. A failed experiment is still useful if it eliminates a bad path.

Aether Abandoned

Hybrid confidence-scored knowledge graph

An early architecture attempted to attach probabilistic confidence scores to nodes in an otherwise explicit knowledge graph. Abandoned once it became clear this reintroduced the same opacity the deterministic approach was meant to avoid.

Closed
Energy Brick Abandoned

Resin-integrated cell layout, first generation

An early cell layout embedded directly in the resin matrix showed unacceptable capacity loss under structural load. The approach was set aside in favor of an isolated-cell architecture within the composite.
