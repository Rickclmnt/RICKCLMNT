Paths to Access
How technology transfer works
01Research Access
Access to technical information, demonstrations, prototypes, or research materials.
02Non-Exclusive Licensing
Permission to use the technology under agreed terms while it remains licensable elsewhere.
03Exclusive Licensing
Exclusive rights within an agreed field, territory, market, or application.
04Acquisition
A complete sale of a technology or its associated intellectual property.

Every project is negotiated individually. Patronage and technology transactions stay separate. Supporting the lab doesn't transfer ownership of its inventions.

Ownership
The work stays independent.

Unless a separate agreement says otherwise, the intellectual property developed through RICKCLMNT remains under my ownership. Patronage provides support, it doesn't transfer ownership.

If you want commercial rights to a technology, that's a separate conversation. Get in touch to start it.
