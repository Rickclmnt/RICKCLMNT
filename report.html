Intelligence Systems⬡

Two approaches to the design of intelligent systems.

The research behind this exhibition explores different approaches to intelligence. From deterministic reasoning and explicit knowledge representation to programmable neurons and adaptive behaviour, each project investigates how intelligent systems can be designed, understood, controlled, and applied.

PTM and 20N are independent technologies with different architectures and objectives.

RESEARCH INDEX / 2026

Exhibit I.
Thought.

PTM — Patrick's Thinking Machine.

PTM is an independent investigation into deterministic artificial intelligence, built around structured knowledge, explicit reasoning, epistemic evaluation, and controlled language expression.

PTM uses deterministic logic to produce answers. Its architecture is designed to make the process behind its conclusions inspectable rather than concealing it behind the black-box generation characteristic of large language models.

The project explores an alternative approach to building AI, centred on what a system knows, how it arrives at conclusions, and how it expresses what it knows.

Exhibit II.
Motion.

20N — Programmable Neural Architecture.

20N (two-ON) explores a programmable neural architecture in which individual neurons can be assigned explicit functional instructions while retaining the capacity to adapt through training.

Rather than treating a neural network solely as a structure whose behaviour emerges through learning, 20N investigates how deliberate programming and adaptation can coexist within a neural system.

With robotics as a primary application, the project examines the potential of neural systems designed for controlled behaviour, adaptability, and specialised functions.

Program first. Improve through training. Programming defines the intended responsibilities. Training improves how well the network performs.

The Creator.


Patrick Clement
Independent Technology Researcher & Software Engineer.

I develop original technologies that investigate alternative approaches to computation, artificial intelligence, and intelligent systems.

My work includes PTM, a deterministic AI system, and 20N, a programmable neural architecture primarily intended for robotics. Both projects reflect an interest in rethinking how intelligent systems are structured, how their behaviour can be controlled, and what new capabilities alternative architectures might enable.

This exhibition is an annual record of my work: a presentation of the technologies developed, the ideas explored, and the progress made.
