Cognitive Systems / Artificial Intelligence

Aether

A deterministic cognitive architecture designed around explicit knowledge, structured reasoning, memory, and controlled uncertainty.

Aether is an attempt to build a cognitive system that can reason from explicit knowledge rather than simply generate plausible answers — inspectable at every step, rather than a black box that happens to be right most of the time.

StatusActive Research
CategoryCognitive Systems / AI
StageArchitecture & Prototype

Legal Intelligence

ALIS

A self-hosted legal intelligence system designed around deterministic processing, explicit rules, structured evidence, and auditable outputs.

ALIS is a proving ground for whether deterministic, rule-based processing can hold up in a domain where explainability and auditability often matter as much as raw capability.

StatusPrototype / Technology Development
CategoryLegal Intelligence
StagePrototype

Materials / Energy

Energy Brick

An investigation into structural material systems capable of integrating energy-related functionality into the built environment.

The objective isn't to make another theoretical material on paper. It is to determine whether a structural composite can actually store and generate meaningful energy without compromising its load-bearing role.

CategoryMaterials / Energy
